WHY VERDANT

Why Verdant. Protection built for the growing season.

01

Weather arrives before paperwork

Farmers need support at the moment of loss, not weeks after claim documents are collected and reviewed.

02

Traditional claims are slow

Manual assessment, intermediaries and uncertainty add cost and delay to every payout decision.

03

Every season needs clear terms

Parametric cover fixes the threshold and payout in advance, so settlement follows an objective rainfall result.

THE PROCESS

How it works. Four simple steps from premium to payout.

01 · PROVIDER

Fund the pool

The provider approves test mUSDC, then the fundPool function transfers it into the insurance vault.

02 · FARMER

Buy cover

The farmer approves the premium and calls createPolicy with location, rainfall threshold, payout and duration.

03 · ORACLE

Report rainfall

The oracle calls fulfillWeatherData with the policy ID and measured rainfall.

04 · SMART CONTRACT

Settle automatically

The contract compares rainfall to the threshold. A below-threshold reading sends the payout automatically.

Rainfall 80 mm

No payout
